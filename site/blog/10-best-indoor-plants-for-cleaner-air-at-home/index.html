10 Best Indoor Plants for Cleaner Air at Home

2025-01-29

Indoor plants beautify your home. They purify the air. The more plants in the house, the more carbon dioxide is absorbed and oxygen is released to improve the air quality. Here are 10 of the best indoor plants that can help you breathe cleaner air at home.

  1. Spider Plant (Chlorophytum Comosum)

It is quite popular indoors. It’s a versatile, strong species. This species has tolerance to harsh conditions. Removing toxins includes the elimination of formaldehyde and xylene. Its characteristics as an air purifier are among the key reasons it finds a place within your indoor walls.

  1. Snake Plant (Sansevieria Trifasciata)

The Snake Plant is a non-demanding, hardy plant that does not require much care. It’s great for new gardeners and efficiently removes harmful chemicals. However, it also makes oxygen at night, so it is an ideal bedroom companion. It is further famous for removing formaldehyde, benzene, and other poisons.

  1. Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum)

The Peace Lily is a very aesthetic and practical flower. It excels at clearing out toxic gases like ammonia, benzene, and formaldehyde. It doesn’t require bright light; indeed, it survives in dim lights. Just let it stay there in your lounge, and this will naturally make the air good to breathe in.

  1. Aloe Vera

Aloe Vera is not only curative but also air-purifying. This fleshy green succulent sucks formaldehyde, benzene, and other harmful toxic chemicals from the air. It has easy care for nature and thrives in bright but indirect sunlight conditions. Aloe Vera is fantastic for skin development, too.

  1. Bamboo Palm (Chamaedorea Seifrizii)

This is an excellent air purification plant, getting rid of toxins such as benzene and formaldehyde. It can tolerate low light, so it is perfectly fine to be used indoors. This plant can also help in increasing humidity levels; therefore, it is ideal for dry seasons.

  1. English Ivy (Hedera Helix)

English Ivy is a good plant used to eliminate airborne mould and other allergens. This plant can be grown in hangers and pots. Moderate to bright indirect light favours the growth of this plant. Purification of air by the plant makes it apt for any indoor environment.

  1. Rubber Plant (Ficus Elastica)

The Rubber Plant is a strong, thriving plant in bright, indirect light. Also, this plant is great at removing formaldehyde and other pollutants. Additionally, this plant can grow quite tall, which makes it an attractive focal point for any room. Large, glossy leaves make it a very elegant addition to your home decoration.

  1. Pothos (Epipremnum Aureum)

Pothos is a hardy, low-maintenance plant. It does extremely well under almost any light and can be grown in almost any environment. This plant does a fantastic job of removing formaldehyde, benzene, and xylene. Additionally, it’s good for hanging baskets or the tabletop.

  1. Dracaena

Dracaena is a very large genus of plants with air-purifying qualities. They are known to remove formaldehyde and benzene from the air. The plant prefers bright, indirect light and infrequent watering. Moreover, this tall, upright growth makes the plant an excellent addition to any room.

  1. Gerbera Daisy

Gerbera Daisy is one of the bright colours and cheerful-looking plants that lighten the room in variegated shades and hues. In addition, it filters out trichloroethylene and benzene toxins efficiently. This plant thrives best in bright indirect lighting, so it requires water daily.
