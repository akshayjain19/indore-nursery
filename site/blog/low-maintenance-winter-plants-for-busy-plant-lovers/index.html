Key takeaways

  • It is the best time for low-maintenance plants, needing less water and maintenance.
  • Indoor plants such as Snake Plant and ZZ Plant are great for busy people.
  • Outdoor plants such as chrysanthemums and hellebores add winter color effortlessly.
  • Buying seasonal plants from nurseries or online ensures healthy growth and convenience.

Plant lovers can find winter a challenging season. During the cold weather, it is difficult to keep vibrant indoor and outdoor plants. But busy plant lovers need not be concerned. To guarantee the enjoyment of greenery without wasting time on its maintenance, it is better to choose low-maintenance winter plants. 

However, you can have a couple of plants within your house or have a garden outside, and there are numerous varieties that can survive even during the cold season. If you visit the best plant nursery in Indore, you can get a variety of seasonal plants.

Why choose low-maintenance plants in winter?

Plants also experience problems such as low sunlight, dry air, and low temperatures during winter. The use of low-maintenance plants is desirable as

  • They need minimal watering.
  • They can survive under the low light conditions.
  • They are cold resistant or thermal resistant.
  • They do not need much pruning or attention.

Additionally, these plants are ideal for busy people who do not have time to spend much time in the garden. Planting them will keep your home or garden greener and active throughout the season.

Top low-maintenance indoor plants

Not only do indoor plants add to the quality of air, but they also add to the mood of your house. The following are some of the low-maintenance winter options:

  1. Snake Plant
  • Grows well in low-light conditions and is able to withstand a lack of water.
  • Fits well in bedrooms or living rooms.
  • The plant needs little care and is therefore suitable for busy plant lovers.
  1. ZZ Plant
  • Very tough and able to endure in nearly any domestic environment.
  • They need water only once in a few weeks.
  • Easily available as online indoor plants in Indore.
  1. Peace Lily
  • White flowers are beautiful and give light to the indoors.
  • Allows poor light and some neglect.
  • It is fresh without having to be sprayed regularly.
  1. Pothos
  • Rapid, versatile, and low-service.
  • Plants in hanging pots or shelves.
  • Plants greenery with no need of constant attention.

Best outdoor plants for winter

Winter does not imply the end of greenery, even in case you have a garden or a balcony. Think of these tough outdoor plants:

  1. Chrysanthemums
  • The winter flowers make any outdoor space colorful.
  • Needs very little watering and attention.
  • Available at online outdoor plants in Indore.
  1. Hellebores
  • Also called Christmas Rose, perfect for cold weather.
  • Can survive frost and snow.
  • Only requires pruning occasionally.
  1. Ornamental Kale
  • Provides winter gardens with texture and color.
  • Hardy and very easy to grow.
  • Perfect where there is a planter or border.
  1. Heather
  • Plants that last long and require little care.
  • Able to endure cold and windy weather.
  • Excellent in adorning garden boundaries or pots.

Tips for caring low-maintenance plants in winter

Even plants that require little attention require attention. They can prosper by following easy rules:

  • Water Carefully: Overwatering can harm plants more than the cold. Water only when the soil is dry.
  • Light Matters: Place indoor plants near windows to ensure they get enough sunlight.
  • Keep Warm: Keep outdoor plants out of the strong winds or frost.
  • Apply Mulch: In the case of outdoor plants, a layer of mulch should be used, which is limited to thin.
  • Clean Leaves: Dust may stop the sunshine. Clean the leaves of indoor plants on a regular basis.

Frequently Asked Questions

  1. Which indoor plants require the least care during winter?

Snake plant, ZZ plant, peace lily, and pothos are ideal to use in winter indoor gardening with low maintenance.

  1. How often should low-maintenance winter plants be watered?

Only water when the soil is dry, typically once every one to two weeks.

  1. Can outdoor plants survive harsh winter conditions?

Yes, cold-tolerant hardy plants such as chrysanthemums, hellebores, ornamental kale, and heather can be left with little or no care.
