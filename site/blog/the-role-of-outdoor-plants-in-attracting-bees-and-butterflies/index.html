Introduction

Outdoor plants are not just beautiful with flowers inside them. They also give bees and butterflies food and shelter, and this is highly important to nature. These small pollinators make flowers bloom and crops bear fruit and seeds. Plants cannot live without them. Good outdoor plants can give bees and butterflies food and shelter. They remain healthy and secure, and they will visit and go to your garden.

However, the plants will remain healthy if the pollinators continue to visit them. Flowers will open wider, and the fruits will get bigger. In climates that are warm, like Indore, the plants that can tolerate summer heat would need to be selected if planted outside. Certain plants are more suitable for this type of climate than others and attract pollinators.

Selecting the appropriate plants and maintaining them benefits your garden and the environment. In this blog, we will provide you with suggestions on how to do it. You will understand what plants should be planted outside during summer and why these are good for butterflies and bees.

Why do bees and butterflies matter?

Bees and butterflies are significant pollinators of the earth. They aid in helping plants reproduce by transferring pollen from one flower to another. This maximizes the amount of fruit and seeds. Without them, most of the crops would die. Bees and butterflies also aid in keeping diversity intact. They aid in not letting the natural conditions become unbalanced. These pollinators are threatened, however, due to pesticides and habitat loss. Organizing the outdoor environment with the appropriate plants positively affects the pollinators. Gardeners, therefore, need to understand how pollinators and plants relate to each other.

How outdoor plants attract pollinators?

Outdoor plants attract bees and butterflies by displaying unique characteristics that they adore.

  • Bright Colors: Bright, bold colors such as yellow, purple, blue, and pink are found appealing for bees and butterflies. Vivid colors are highly noticeable from far away and facilitate them finding flowers easily.
  • Smell: The flowers smell nice and sweet. They have a scent that is a cue to invite the bees and butterflies to visit the flowers from far away. The nicer and stronger the scent, the greater the chances of visiting by pollinators.
  • Nectar and Pollen: Flowers give flowers nectar, a sweet beverage, and pollen, a protein. Both are required and keep bees and butterflies healthy and well. Pollinators are unable to exist without them.
  • Flower Shape: Flower shape contributes as well. Flowers are tubular and open and exposed. There are flower shapes that assist bees and butterflies in nectar and pollen collection more.

Additionally, this requires the timing of the flowers’ blooms. Summer-blooming flowers are required as a food source during the time the pollinators are active. Therefore, choosing the best summer-flowering outdoor plants in Indore will assist in supporting and maintaining local bee and butterfly populations.

Best outdoor plants for summer in Indore

Indore has hot summers and so needs heat-tolerant plants. Some of the best outdoor plants for summer in Indore that are bee- and butterfly-friendly are listed below

  • Marigold: Orange flowers that are attractive to many pollinators.
  • Zinnia: Delicate and rich, ideal for butterflies.
  • Lantana: Hard shrub with cluster flowers that are attractive to bees.
  • Sunflower: Provides pollen and nectar with large but closed flowers.
  • Bougainvillea: colored bracts and water, which attract bees and butterflies.

These flowers are ideal for the summer season. Thus, it is a great option for a person who wants to design a pollinator garden in Indore.

Creating a pollinator-friendly garden

In order to design a bee and butterfly ecosystem in your garden, the following are useful:

  • Plant Variety: Plant some varieties of flowers of different shapes, colors, and flowering periods.
  • Avoid Pesticides: Pesticides kill pollinators and render plants weaker
  • Provide Water: Shallow trays of water keep dry insects in a good state of hydration
  • Make Shelter: Grow small shrubs or trees to provide shelter.
  • Leave Some Wild Places: Leave some area for nature to grow. This is good for a lot of types of pollinators.

Moreover, your garden will be able to attract and retain plenty of pollinators. This is good for the environment and your plants.
