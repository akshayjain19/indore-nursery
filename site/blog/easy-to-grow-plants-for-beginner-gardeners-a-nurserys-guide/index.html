1. Marigolds
Marigolds are vibrant, low-maintenance annuals that bloom abundantly throughout the growing season. Their cheerful blooms come in various shades of yellow, orange, and red, adding a pop of color to any garden. Marigolds are resilient and can thrive in sunny locations with well-drained soil, making them an excellent choice for beginner gardeners. 2. Lavender
Lavender is a fragrant and visually appealing herb known for its calming scent and stunning purple blooms. This perennial plant thrives in sunny spots with well-drained soil, making it an excellent addition to garden borders or container gardens. Lavender is relatively drought-tolerant and attracts pollinators, making it an ideal choice for novice gardeners seeking a low-maintenance, yet rewarding, plant. Basil is a versatile herb that is easy to grow and offers a myriad of culinary uses. Whether it’s classic sweet basil, spicy Thai basil, or fragrant lemon basil, these aromatic herbs thrive in sunny locations with well-drained soil. Beginner gardeners can enjoy the satisfaction of growing their own fresh herbs forcooking, making basil a popular choice for gardens and kitchen windowsills alike. Peppermint is a vigorous, spreading herb that is well-suited for beginner gardeners. This aromatic herb thrives in moist, well-drained soil and can be cultivated in containers to prevent its invasive growth. 4. Strawberries
Strawberries are a delightful addition to any garden, offering sweet, juicy fruits throughout the growing season.
