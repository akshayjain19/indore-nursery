Why are indoor plants perfect for apartments?

Apartment living means little space. But indoor plants can be placed nearly everywhere. They provide color and texture without much space. Moreover, plants cleanse the air. This provides a cleaner living environment in flats. Succulents require minimal water and light. Therefore, they are ideal for apartment living.

Plants also provide a soothing ambiance. When it is a hectic day, green plants can minimize anxiety. This is the reason why most people opt for plants for home decor in Indore. Plants, apart from being beautiful, have numerous health benefits. For example:

  • They are a means of increasing oxygen levels within the house.
  • They minimize dust and toxins in the air.
  • They enhance mood and creativity.

Due to such advantages, most apartments now have green corners. If you wish to get onto this bandwagon, read further to discover the best indoor plants.

Top indoor plants for small spaces

Choosing a proper plant is of importance when space is limited. Following are some of the most appropriate houseplants to be kept in apartments and flats:

  1. Snake Plant (Sansevieria)

Taking care of snake plants is not difficult and flexible. They are low maintenance and can grow in poor light with minimal water. They also have the ability to clean indoor air. Their thin, long leaves look sophisticated in any corner.

  1. Spider plant

Spider plants grow rapidly and tolerate a range of light. They are beautiful in hanging baskets or on a shelf. They also purify indoor air by removing pollutants.

  1. Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)

Pothos are simple to grow and maintain. They will also perform well in low light and add a pleasant touch to their vines with immoral water, and their vines.

  1. ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)

Zz is very difficult to kill plants. They love less light and inadvertently water. Their waxy green leaves create a shiny appearance in your area.

  1. Success and Cacti

Successants and cacti take some space and water. They also come in all sizes and sizes. These plants are perfect for windows and workspaces.

  1. Peace Lily (Spathifilm)

Peace Lily produces stunning white flowers. Peace lilies can thrive in low light conditions and require moderate water. In addition to their appearance, they remove toxic chemicals from the air.

  1. Rubber Plant (Ficus Elastic)

The rubber plant claims huge, shiny leaves. Rubber plants can be grown indoors in shade. Your apartment receives a splash of tropics with these plants.

How to choose the right indoor plant for your flat?

Plant choice is also influenced by your flat’s climate. The following are some of the determinants:

  • Availability of Light: Take note of the light entering your flat daily.
  • Space: Choose plants that complement your available space.
  • Care Level: Decide how much time you have available to spend on plant care.
  • Purpose: Do you only need the plants for looks or purification of air?

Furthermore, if your apartment receives very little sunlight, snake plants or ZZ plants would be ideal. For spaces that receive sunlight, cacti and succulents would be ideal. Hanging plants also save space on the floor and are beautiful. Most such plants can be purchased online indoor plants in Indore. This gives you options without even leaving your house. 

Tips to care for indoor plants in apartments

Proper care makes houseplants robust and healthy. Utilize the below simple tips:

  • Water houseplants according to their needs. Don’t overwater.
  • Plant in well-draining soil to prevent root rot.
  • Place houseplants at the right light positions.
  • Clean leaves with dust off occasionally.
  • Turn the plant so that all the surfaces get light.
  • Utilize natural fertilizers for growth.

Additionally, continue to observe your plants from time to time. Look for any insects or signs of stress. With care, your plants will flourish and make your apartment look stunning.
