However, the positive news is that there are a large number of indoor plants that can be strong enough to endure and grow properly even during winter. Why choose the right indoor plants for winter matters?

Cold weather is not liked by all plants. Others cease to grow, others lose foliage, or become feeble. That is why it is highly important to select plants that are adapted to cooler temperatures.

Indoor plants that can withstand winter are very advantageous:

  • They enhance the indoor air quality in closed-window months.
  • They bring warmth and beauty to drab winter areas.
  • They do not require as much watering as summer plants.
  • Even when there is low sunlight, they remain healthy.

Additionally, they are simpler to maintain, and as such, can be used by busy households. Though winter can make the growth of plants slow down, the correct decision keeps greenery alive and fresh even in the house.

Best indoor winter plants that can handle low temperatures

These are some of the most suitable indoor winter plants that grow in low temperatures. These are plants that are powerful, beautiful, and do not require a lot of care.

  • Snake Plant

This plant is very powerful, and it can withstand cool rooms. It does not require a lot of sunlight or water. Besides, it will clean indoor air, and it is ideal in bedrooms and living rooms.

  • Peace Lily

Peace lilies can be cultivated in low light and cooler environments. They have beautiful white flowers that light up interiors. Nevertheless, they require frequent but sparse watering.

  • Spider Plant

Spider plants are very tolerant of temperature. They are easy to grow, even during winter, and they appear good in hanging pots. Also, they contribute to the enhancement of air quality.

  • Aloe Vera

Aloe is a slow-growing plant that is very hardy even in the house. It likes cool rooms that have some sunlight. Moreover, aloe leaves can be used in taking care of the skin.

  • Pothos

The pothos plants are quite simple to maintain. They develop in low-light conditions and endure cold indoor conditions. Thus, they fit well in offices and homes.

  • Rubber Plant

The leaves of this plant are thick and hence retain moisture. It is able to endure winter dryness in a better manner than most plants. In addition, it gives a room a striking green appearance.

Simple care tips to keep winter plants healthy

Caring about the plants during winter is not hard provided that you observe several simple rules. Even in cold months the growth is reduced, but with proper care the plants remain healthy.

Important tips include:

  • Water infrequently, because the soil becomes dry in winter.
  • Keep vegetation out of cold drafts and open windows.
  • Put the plants in indirect sunlight, taking a few hours a day.
  • Wipe leaves with a clean cloth to get dust off.
  • Apply light fertilizer when necessary.

Additionally, do not overwater; this is the most widespread mistake in winter. Soil moisture should be checked before watering, therefore.

How to choose the right plants for your home?

The selection of the appropriate indoor winter plants is based on the environment in your home. Certain houses remain warmer, and others are colder. In addition, the availability of light is also important.

Before selecting plants, consider these points:

  • Daytime room temperature.
  • Natural light supply.
  • Space for plant growth
  • Time available to attend to plants.
