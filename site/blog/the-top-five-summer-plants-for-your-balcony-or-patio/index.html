The Top Five Summer Plants for Your Balcony or Patio

2024-12-09 · Indore Nursery Team

Summer is here, and now it is time to give a nice makeover to your balcony or patio. This place may become more beautiful with the addition of beautiful plants, which further create a refreshing atmosphere. If you are thinking of sprinkling life on your balcony, here are the top five summer plants that grow well under the sun.

  1. Petunias

Petunias are among the brightest flowering plants of summer. They come in various colours, mostly pink, purple, and white. They do love the sun a lot, and they bloom outstandingly when placed in a sunny spot. These plants are so effortless to look after and can easily be planted in hanging baskets or pots. Due to the vibrancy of their flowers, they surely will give colour to your balcony or patio.

Besides, they are low-maintenance and require watering only in case the soil feels dry. These flowers can tolerate heat and, therefore, are suitable for summer.

  1. Geraniums

Another great choice is the geranium for your outdoor space. Geraniums provide attractive flowers in colours of red, pink, and white to bring a touch of class to any balcony or patio. They are ideal for sunny sections of the house that get a minimum of six hours of sunlight daily.

However, Geranium plants have fairly decent resistance to high temperatures during summer. Prime requirements for these plants are good drainage and consistent irrigation. Not to forget, the pleasant fragrance can make your space smell great. Geranium plants work quite well in hanging baskets, pots, and flower beds.

  1. Lavender

If you need both beauty and fragrance, then lavender is the way to go. It does exceptionally well during hot, dry summers and is excellent for the season. Therefore, the flowers of lavender are purple-coloured and known for their sweet fragrance, rendering freshness and relaxation to an outdoor space.

Moreover, Lavender requires well-drained soil and plenty of sunlight. It is drought-resistant, which means you need not frequent its watering. If you are looking for plants for home decor in Indore, then lavender will be a great addition because it has an appealing beauty.

  1. Succulents

Succulents are one of the most current plants for an outdoor balcony or patio garden. The leaves of succulents store water in them, making them resistant to drought conditions. In addition, succulents come in many shapes and sizes, adding your personal touch to your outdoor space.

Furthermore, they require very little maintenance and can tolerate as much direct sunlight as possible. Succulents are great plants for very busy people since they require less care. You also could plant them in different colours and textures to make a beautiful but low-maintenance garden.

  1. Bougainvillea

The flowers are very bright-coloured and beautiful in the Bougainvillea plant. These flowers come in different colours ranging from pink to purple and then to red and orange. It prefers full sun and well-drained soil for good growth. Because it is a climber, you may let it grow over the trellis or wall.

The plant of Bougainvillea adores hot weather and thus is ideal for the summer season. It creates a tropical view for your balcony or patio. However, it needs regular pruning for shape and to prevent it from becoming too wild, meaning that it should be planted to have a colourful, tropical atmosphere.

Caring for Your Summer Plants

Here are some easy ways to make sure your summer plants remain healthy:

Watering: Most plants in summer require being watered regularly. However, this should not be overdone as it may lead to root rot.

Soil: Use well-draining soil. Good drainage keeps the soil free from waterlogging and roots consequently stay healthy.

Sunlight: These summer plants want and need much sunlight. Place them, therefore, in a spot with much sun.

Fertilising: Fertilise your plants regularly to ensure growth. Organic fertilisers work best for plants.

Pruning: Dead and faded flowers need to be pruned regularly to keep the plant in bloom and looking its best.
