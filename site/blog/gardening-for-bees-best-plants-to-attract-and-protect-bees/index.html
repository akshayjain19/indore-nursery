Gardening for Bees: Best Plants to Attract and Protect Bees

2024-11-08 · Indore Nursery Team

Bees play a very important role in our environment. Without bees, flowers could not be pollinated, and crops would suffer. Therefore, developing bee-friendly gardens is very important. With the appropriate plants, you can attract bees and thus protect them.

Why Bees Matter in Your Garden?

Bees pollinate a third of our food crops. They are responsible for the reproduction of flowers and plants. By cultivating bee-friendly plants you help increase the population of bees. Moreover, these plants make your garden more beautiful and colourful.

Best Plants to Attract Bees

Nothing is as important in making the correct environment for bees as the right plant selection. Some plants bloom at different seasons so that there is food for them all year round. Additionally, it’s known that native plants are more appealing to bees. Here are some of the most efficient plants you can grow to attract bees.

Lavender

Bees love lavender. It is excellent for its fragrance and blooms colourful flowers. Lavender blooms for several months, providing constant food for the bees. Therefore, you can either plant in pots or directly to the ground. Suitable for indoor as well as outdoor spaces, lavender is available for the enthusiast to use in a wide range of different ways.

Sunflowers

Sunflowers are ideal for attracting bees. Their large, bright heads are full of pollen, making them the absolute best choice for bees. Moreover, they grow very easily and require little maintenance. Bees love their prolific pollen, so you can guarantee that the sunflowers in your garden will be very happy.

Bee Balm

Bee balm is another top one. It is an extremely attractive flower to the pollinators. The bright flowers bloom in the summer and provide bees with a good supply of nectar, so their supplement is added to the garden as well.

Coneflowers

Coneflowers are among the easiest plants to grow while being extremely hardy. They appeal to bees by the bright colour that they attract throughout the summer and fall seasons. Furthermore, they enhance the beauty of your garden due to its bold purple petals.

Thyme

Thyme is a herb that beautifies your foods and attracts bees too. It grows extremely well in pots, thus perfect for home gardens. Besides, thyme blooms with small flowers that bees can never resist.

Creating a Bee-Friendly Garden

If you do not consider the right way of arranging them, then the choice of plants will be inappropriate. This is how one can make the garden bee-friendly by combining flowering plants that attract the bees all through the year.

Choose Native Plants

Native plants fit naturally into your local environment. Bees are familiar with them thus, bees are more likely to find their way there. They also tend to consume much less water and require much less maintenance.

Provide Water

Bees need to live in water, especially on hot summer days. You may give your bees water either on a dish-shaped plate with stones placed in the garden or a well shallow and filled with clear, cool water.

Avoid Pesticides

These pesticides are poisonous to bees. If there is an option to use them, then at least select organic pesticides. However, it is always wise to avoid them. Organic gardening practices are safer for your plants and also safer for your bees.

Plant in Clusters

Attracts bees in clusters of the same plant. If planted as a cluster rather than spreading out individual flowers, it makes it easier for bees to locate food. 

Plants for Both Indoor and Outdoor Gardens

You can surely have a bee-friendly environment in your home, even if you live in an apartment or don’t have much outdoor space.
