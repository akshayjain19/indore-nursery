Best Varieties of Hanging Plants for Low Light Homes

2025-12-05

Bright sunlight is not always necessary to create a relaxing and refreshing home. A lot of individuals reside in apartments or houses that do not have natural lighting. Nevertheless, the fact that there is low light does not imply that you cannot have beautiful greenery. Indeed, there are plants that grow well in those areas, particularly when they are in hanging planters. These plants do not only save space but also provide any room with softness and warmth.

However, when trying to find a solution to bringing nature indoors, many individuals seek the best plant nursery in Indore to get healthy plants that fit their homes. This blog will tell you what kind of hanging plants are better planted in low-light areas and how you can maintain them to look fresh all year round.

Why are hanging plants perfect for low light homes?

Hanging plants add liveness to areas where ordinary plants might not be fitting. In addition, they provide height, movement, and texture to a room without occupying the floor space. Even though there are plants that require direct sunlight, there are numerous types that can be used in dark rooms, bedrooms, or corridors.

Additionally, hanging plants make the interior spaces look natural and welcoming. Their trailing vines give a soft and calming appearance, which is best suited for home decor. Therefore, the selection of appropriate plants may help to entirely change your interior world.

Top low-light hanging plants

These are the most dependable and lovely hanging plants in low lights. All of them are simple to maintain and are beautiful even in areas with minimal sunlight.

  1. Pothos (Money Plant)

Pothos is a low-light hanging plant that is easy to grow. It has long vines that grow very well with little attention. Additionally, it can still last long even when you forget to water it for a few days. This plant is able to occupy corners and bring a natural, flowing feel to any room.

  1. English Ivy

English ivy thrives very well in the shade and is very beautiful when the vines are permitted to dangle off shelves or baskets. Though it matures slowly in color, it is still green and fresh. It can be used in the living room, bathrooms, or the entrance.

  1. Heartleaf Philodendron

This plant is associated with heart-shaped leaves and a rich growth. It can adapt easily to various light conditions, such as low light. Moreover, it produces long trailing vines that transform any room into a warm and cozy one.

  1. Spider Plant

Spider plants are traditional house favorites. They thrive in shady conditions and are very pretty when planted in hanging baskets. They also make small baby plantlets, which hang on the mother plant, giving it a layered appearance.

  1. ZZ Plant (Hanging Form)

The hanging type of ZZ plant is becoming popular, although it is usually kept in pots. It works with low light very well and requires minimal care. Moreover, its shiny leaves are a contemporary and fashionable way to decorate the homes.

  1. String of Hearts

The leaves of this plant are heart-shaped and are placed on fine trailing stems. It prefers diffused light and thrives well in places that are at the windows that receive soft sunlight. Furthermore, it gives any corner a romantic and charming appearance.

  1. Ferns (Certain Varieties)

There are also types of fern that thrive in the dark, like Boston Fern, and are beautiful in hanging pots. Their feathered fronds add motion and cushiness to a room. They are easy to maintain, though they prefer humidity.

  1. Tradescantia

Tradescantia is a colored-leaf plant and thrives even in dark areas. It gives it a bright appearance and does not require direct sunlight. Moreover, it flows well and occupies blank areas easily.

Tips to keep low light hanging plants healthy

The following are the basic care tips that you should remember to make your plants grow even in low light:

  • Water carefully: The majority of low-light plants dislike excess water. Water the soil slightly between watering.
  • Rotate plants: Rotate your hanging planters after every few weeks to ensure all sides receive an equal amount of light.
  • Clean leaves: Clean leaves very softly so as to breathe and grow well.
  • Select the correct potting mix: This is a light and well-draining potting mix that keeps the roots healthy.
  • Keep out of the way of heat: Do not put plants near heaters or in front of direct air currents from the air conditioner.

However, these easy measures assist in keeping your hanging plants fresh and make them the best plants to use in decorating your homes indoors.

Frequently Asked Questions

  1. Which hanging plants grow best in low light?

Pothos, English ivy, heartleaf philodendron, spider plant, and ferns are all excellent plants that grow in low-light indoor locations.

  1. How often should I water low-light hanging plants?

Water once or twice in a month, and leave the soil to dry out in between watering sessions.

  1. Do hanging plants improve indoor air quality in low-light homes?

Yes, numerous dark hanging plants are natural indoor air purifiers that make living healthier.

  1. Can low-light hanging plants survive without natural sunlight?

They are able to endure with soft indirect light or artificial indoor light that is applied on a daily basis
