Top 10 Hanging Plants to Beautify Your Balcony

2025-02-06

A balcony is more than just an outdoor space; it is a personal sanctuary for relaxation and connecting with nature. Adding hanging plants to your balcony will instantly uplift the ambience, making your space beautiful while aerating it at the same time. In this blog, we will discuss the top 10 hanging plants that can make your balcony a green haven.

  1. English Ivy (Hedera Helix)

English Ivy is a versatile and low-maintenance plant. It thrives in indirect sunlight and easily fits your balcony. Its trailing vines create a lush, green waterfall effect. Additionally, it is excellent in improving air quality.

  1. Spider Plant (Chlorophytum comosum)

Spider Plant is a great choice for hanging baskets as it is less fussy in its care requirements and, thus, excellent for beginners. Its long arching leaves, which are always sporting baby plantlets, create an attractive appeal. It grows well in indirect sunlight.

  1. Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)

Pothos, or Devil’s Ivy, is an evergreen climber. Its lovely heart-shaped leaves are olive green upturned with golden yellow variegation. However, the lovely thing about pothos is how easily they grow and tolerate both bright and low-light conditions. It is excellent for hanging baskets or planters.

  1. Boston Fern (Nephrolepis exaltata)

If you want to add a little elegance to your balcony, the Boston Fern is a great choice. This plant loves high humidity, but it does well with low to indirect sunlight. Its delicate, feathery fronds cascade beautifully, so it’s well-suited as a hanging plant. It’s also said to purify indoor air.

  1. String of Pearls (Senecio rowleyanus)

The String of Pearls is perfect for a different and beautiful appearance. The plant’s trailing vines with small, pearl-like beads are very eye-catching. It prefers bright, indirect sunlight and dry soil. But it requires some watering occasionally, so it is not that hard to take care of.

  1. Coleus (Solenostemon scutellarioides)

Coleus is a colourful, vibrant foliage plant. The leaves of this plant come in a variety of shades such as purple, red, pink, and green. It requires partial sunlight and can instantly brighten up your balcony. However, Coleus is low maintenance and can be grown in hanging baskets.

  1. Fuchsia

Fuchsia is a flowering plant distinguished by its bright, bell-shaped flowers. The cascading blooms make this plant a showstopper on any balcony. Fuchsia prefers partial to full sun and should be watered regularly. Fuchsia will return the favour with colourful flowers all through the season.

  1. Succulent Plants

Succulents are low-maintenance plants that can be used for hanging baskets. They add texture and interest to your balcony due to their unique shapes and colours. Plants like Echeveria, Sedum, and Aloe Vera are ideal for this purpose. Therefore, succulents thrive in bright, indirect sunlight and require minimal watering.

  1. Money Plant (Pothos)

Many cultures associate the Money Plant with prosperity and good luck. It is a low-maintenance plant that produces attractive, glossy leaves. The plant can be grown in a hanging pot or along a trellis. Money plants require indirect sunlight and moderate watering.

  1. Morning Glory

Morning Glory is a fast-growing climbing plant that will beautify your balcony. Whether it’s colourful, trumpet-shaped flowers or anything else, it will definitely draw attention to you. It loves the sun and should be watered regularly. Morning Glory blooms in the morning hours with a fresh look in your space.

Choosing the Right Hanging Plant for Your Balcony

Selecting the right plants for your balcony will depend on the lighting and space available. While some plants can thrive in direct sunlight, others prefer to grow in shaded areas. For example, the English Ivy prefers indirect sunlight, while the Morning Glory needs full sun. Furthermore, the available space on your balcony will determine the size and number of hanging plants you can add.
