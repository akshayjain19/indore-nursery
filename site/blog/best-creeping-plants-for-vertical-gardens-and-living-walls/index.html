Introduction

Vertical gardens add charm to empty walls. They introduce nature into houses. Most city houses do not have garden space outside. For this reason, vertical gardens prove to be a clever and eco-friendly solution. Creeping plants fill up most of these walls with green. They stretch, cover, and create living art without any effort. Selecting plants is key to beauty and wellness. This blog guides you in selecting plants best suited for creeping. You will also learn care tips and tricks to garden successfully.

What are vertical gardens and living walls?

Vertical gardens are a clever way of growing plants above frames or walls. They save space and enhance the looks of any space. You can design them outdoors or indoors, even above small spaces such as entranceways, balconies, or patios. These gardens are best suited for people who have limited ground space.

Besides, some individuals also grow herbs and vegetables vertically for domestic consumption. Special irrigation and support systems are typically employed for living walls. They’re not only aesthetically pleasing—everyone stays calm in their company—they also cleanse the air. A well-planned vertical garden brings nature into your life. Plant selection is everything. As long as proper maintenance is provided, your vertical garden can be a green and healthy component of your home.

Benefits of using best creeping plants

Creeping vegetation grows naturally and in a charming manner on walls. Vines hang long in all directions. They have flowers on some of them, but others have thick green leaves. These are a few of the benefits:

  • Hide ugly or bare walls instantly.
  • Provide shade and cold weather.
  • Act as natural privacy screens.
  • Attract butterflies and birds.
  • Kill noises and clean air pollutants.

These benefits make creeping plants suitable garden options. However, the majority of creeping plants are also low maintenance.

Best creeping plants for vertical gardens

Let us take a look at the best of them. All of the plants hereunder can be utilized in vertical gardens. Their characteristics vary depending on sunlight, water, and looks.

  1. English Ivy (Hedera helix)

English ivy is a year-round favorite wall climber. Dark green, good-looking leaves adorn it. It can tolerate shade or part-sunlight. It requires minimal maintenance once established. It is best used for indoor and outdoor wall gardens.

  1. Golden Pothos (Epipremnum aureum)

Golden Pothos has low care and is a quick grower. It produces beautiful, shiny, heart-shaped leaves. It cleans the air and is well suited for interior spaces. Provide support structures where it can climb delicately.

  1. Creeping Fig (Ficus pumila)

Creeping Fig is ideal for green walls with texture. It produces very small leaves that densely cover surfaces. It thrives in moist, shaded places. Pruning induces fast growth. Add to small and large vertical gardens.

  1. Climbing Roses

Climbing roses provide flowers and fragrance. They require full sun and annual maintenance. They’re worth the hassle with their blooms, though. Train them on metal trellises or wood frames. Perfect for exterior living walls.

  1. Jasmine (Jasminum spp.)

Jasmine scents rooms with sweet fragrance and refined delicacy. Jasmine grows rapidly and can climb with ease. There are varieties that flower year-round in the tropics. It also brings pollinators to your garden.

  1. Bougainvillea

Bougainvillea is gaudy, bold, and showy. It is a fast grower in full sun. Trim to maintain shape. Utilize outdoor vertical gardens for dramatic effects.

  1. Sweet Potato Vine (Ipomoea batatas)

This vine has gorgeous, multicolored foliage. Thrives in warm and light conditions. In lime, bronze, and dark purple shades. Ideal for balcony walls or hanging baskets.

  1. Passionflower (Passiflora spp.)

Passionflower provides exotic blooms and tropical style. Fast to grow where it’s sunny and naturally climbing. Some also yield edible fruit. A showy option for vertical gardens.

  1. Morning Glory (Ipomoea purpurea)

Morning glory vines bloom every day. They like the sun and produce really quickly. Their blue, pink, or white blooms light up any wall. Additionally, they are bee and butterfly magnets.

How to choose the right plant?

The ideal plant selection is location- and need-oriented. Consider the following:

  • Test light levels like full sun or part shade.
  • Be familiar with the climate and seasons.
  • Select low-maintenance plants if you are a novice gardener.
  • Combine flowering and foliage plants for a lush look.
  • Make sure the wall support can hold the weight of the plant.

Tips for vertical garden success

There is certain management that must be done in having a vertical garden. Certain tips that prove helpful are as follows:

  • Choose robust support structures or frames.
  • Water liberally with drip irrigation.
  • Fertilize monthly to induce healthy growth.
  • Prune occasionally to prevent overgrowth.
  • Pull out dead leaves in order not to allow chances for pest and disease infestation.
  • Plant in clumps to enhance garden health and appearance.
