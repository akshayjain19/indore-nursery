Top Landscaping Plant Trends for 2025: Whats In and Whats Out

2025-01-15 · Indore Nursery Team

Trends in landscaping always evolve. And before 2025, it seems like some new plant selections will shape our outdoor environments. In this blog, we shall discuss the best landscaping plants in 2025. Then we shall have the plants on their way down. Be it for designing a garden from scratch or renewing your garden, these suggestions will bring you up to date.

What’s In: Popular Landscaping Plants for 2025

As the interest in sustainability grows, some plants are becoming popular in landscaping. These plants provide beauty, functionality, and environmental benefits. Here are some top choices for 2025.

  1. Drought-Tolerant Plants

Drought-tolerant plants are now becoming popular. With the growing awareness of water conservation, these plants thrive with minimal water. Examples include lavender, succulents, and agave. Moreover, these plants add texture and colour to your landscape.

  1. Native Plants

For any landscape, native plants are a great choice. They require less maintenance and attract local wildlife. Besides these, they adapt better to local climates, therefore saving on chemicals in the form of fertilizers and pesticides. Most garden centres, the best plant nursery in Indore included, offer a wide variety of native plants.

  1. Vertical Gardens

Another trend in vogue is vertical gardening. This is best suited for smaller spaces or cities. Ivy, ferns, and climbing roses are good choices for vertical gardens. Additionally, it can give a unique and contemporary look to the landscaping.

  1. Edible Landscaping

The more edible plants people grow in home gardens, the better it is. The integration of vegetables, fruits, and herbs in landscaping designs has become more common. Plants like tomatoes, spinach, and strawberries look amazing and serve as a practical means. Therefore, this is a trend that supports beauty and sustainability at the same time.

  1. Low-Maintenance Ground Covers

Creeping thyme and moss are becoming popular ground covers. They spread easily and require minimal maintenance. They can also suppress weeds and reduce the need for mowing.

  1. Indoor-Outdoor Plant Integration

The days when plants were indoors are over. Now, people combine the inside and outside areas of a home using plants like ferns, philodendrons, and palms. Therefore, such plants can be used in an outdoor pot or a hanging basket. There are a variety of indoor plants in Indore online for your reference.

What’s Out: Declining Landscaping Trends

Some of the landscaping trends are becoming less popular as others are taking over. Here are some of the few plant selections that are slowly becoming obsolete in 2025.

  1. Exotic Invasive Species

Exotic invasive species, including bamboo and ivy, are slowly disappearing from the gardens. These plants easily occupy all garden spaces and can be hazardous to local ecosystems. Besides, they are usually demanding high maintenance to stay in check. This is the reason gardeners opt for native and non-invasive plants.

  1. Overly Decorative Plants

Overly decorative plants are no longer in vogue. These plants may look beautiful, but they require a lot of care. People are now opting for simple, low-maintenance plants that provide beauty with minimal effort.

  1. Water-Consuming Plants

With the increasing concern of water scarcity, water-intensive plants are no longer in vogue. Furthermore, these plants require too much watering, which is unsustainable in many areas. For this reason, most gardeners focus on plants that require less water, such as succulents and native species.
