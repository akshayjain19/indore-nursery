Introduction

A Zen garden is a serene means of introducing tranquility to your backyard. It makes use of the materials of natural elements such as pebbles, sand, water, and landscaping plants. The basic materials combine to form a soothing environment. Landscaping plants introduce life, hues, and softness to the garden. They neutralize the rigidity of pebbles and stones.

What is a zen garden? 

A Zen garden, or Japanese rock garden, is a peaceful area for meditation and reflection. It leverages nature to create balance and harmony. Additionally, Zen gardens consist of

  • Rock and gravel designs
  • Dry water or stream
  • Plants and trees that are outside

However, zen gardens are minimalistic and symbolic. They promote relaxation and consciousness.

Why use outdoor plants in a zen garden?

Outdoor plants contribute to the beauty and peace of a Zen Garden. They show natural color and beauty in the garden. Plants also calm the rigorous appearance of rocks and gravel. Some plants provide shade and cover small plants. When the wind blows, the leaves whisper and contribute to a calm effect. 

Therefore, this calm movement helps in resting and getting closer to nature. It is important in a Zen Garden. Nature contributes significantly to peace and harmony. For that reason, someone needs to select the appropriate plants for the outside. Additionally, select easily maintained plants that complement the pure appearance of the garden. The appropriate plant will give a special touch to your Zen Garden.

Choosing the right outdoor plants

Not every plant is best for a Zen garden. You require plants that are low-maintenance and add to the peaceful appearance of the garden. Below are some of the best outdoor plants from the best plant nursery in Indore utilized in Zen gardens:

  • Bamboo: Represents strength and durability. It is extremely tall and slender in size.
  • Japanese Maple: Has bright red or green leaves with lovely shapes.
  • Moss: Spreads on ground or rocks, producing a soft, green carpeted effect.
  • Azaleas: Provides nice color blooms but requires a place of partial shade.
  • Pine Trees: Symbolize longevity and peace of mind.

Considerations while Selecting Plants:

  • Weather conditions and climate
  • Size and growth of the plant
  • Amount of care required
  • Textured and colored

Steps to design your zen garden with outdoor plants

Follow the below easy steps to design your own Zen garden using outdoor plants:

  1. Plan Your Space

Measure your garden space. Decide where you would like to have plants, rocks, and pathways.

  1. Prepare the Ground

Remove weeds and level the ground. Add sand or gravel where necessary.

  1. Select a Focal Point

Select a focal point such as a rock, tree, or water dish. This attracts the eye.

  1. Add Rocks and Gravel

Group rocks together to create islands or mountains. Rake gravel into waves.

  1. Plant Your Garden Plants

Plant plants gently around paths and rocks. Experiment with various heights and textures of plants.

  1. Add Finishing Touches

Insert lanterns, benches, or bamboo fences for ornament.

  1. Water and Maintenance

Water plants and trim dropped leaves on a regular basis to maintain your garden’s cleanliness.

Tips to maintain your zen garden

A Zen garden is always in need of care to maintain its appearance. Here are some maintenance tips:

  • Water plants when they need it.
  • Shear plants to keep them shaped.
  • Kill weeds and dead leaves from time to time.
  • Rake gravel or sand weekly to maintain patterns.
  • Check plants for pests or diseases.

By remembering these tips, your Zen garden will be a peaceful oasis for years to come.
