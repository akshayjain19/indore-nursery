Defining Frost Dates: Frost Dates can be defined as the average dates of the first and last occurrence of freezing temperatures in a specific region . Frost Dates play a very crucial role in deciding when it is safe to start planting or when  to prepare for the winter chills. Determining local Frost Dates :Those factors which are responsible for frost dates are geography , altitudes and proximity of water bodies . You can get the idea of frost dates through online resources such as weather forecast  agriculture extensions or local weather stations . These resources give pinpoint ideas related to local weather conditions as a result which helps you to manage your nursery activities more efficiently and the chances of successful nursery management will increase.  Early v/s Late frost :It is very crucial to understand the difference between the early and late frost as they both are pole opposite in nature. These early frost can catch plants off-guard, potentially causing damage to the tender vegetation . In contrast, late frost caused a threat to the plant which started to bloom . Discuss and manage your nursery accordingly and beware of these distinctions to protect nursery aids by developing strategies from unexpected temperature drops.
