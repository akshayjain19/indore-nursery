Why do people grow money plants indoors?

Money plants are popular as they are simple to cultivate and are beautiful in the house. Besides, they enhance the quality of air and do not demand much effort.

Indoor money plants are preferred by people due to the following:

  • They can survive in low light.
  • They need less water.
  • They grow in soil or water.
  • They fit in modern houses and limited areas.

Additionally, they are good plants for home decor in Indore because of the fresh green appearance and the ability to be styled in different ways.

What type of money plant grows best indoors?

Money plants do not grow equally fast. There are other varieties that are more adapted to indoor environments and grow more quickly.

The best indoor varieties are:

  • Golden Money Plant
  • Marble Queen
  • Neon Money Plant

When these types are kept indoors, they develop long vines and healthy leaves. Hence, it becomes easier and quicker to select the appropriate type at the beginning of the growth.

However, the experienced growers at Indorenursery would always advise using strong-rooted cuttings rather than the weak stems.

How much light does a money plant need indoors?

The most significant factor of fast growth is light. Nevertheless, the leaves may be burnt by direct sunlight.

Optimal indoor lighting conditions:

  • Bright indirect sunlight
  • Close to a window with a filtering curtain.
  • Avoid dark corners.

Despite the fact that money plants can survive in low light, they can grow faster only when there is sufficient brightness. Moreover, weekly rotation of the plant makes sure that the growth is even all around.

Which soil helps money plants grow faster?

The quality of soil has a direct impact on the strength of roots and rate of growth. Poor soil traps water and brings about root rot.

Optimal soil combination with indoor money plant:

  • Garden soil – 40%
  • Coco peat – 30%
  • Compost or vermicompost – 30%

This combination permits air circulation and removes surplus water. Therefore, roots become deeper and stronger.

Furthermore, Indore residents who order from an online nursery in Indore should never plant without first checking the suggestions of the soil quality.

How often should you water an indoor money plant?

The most popular mistake is overwatering. Money plants are suitable in slightly dry soil.

Correct watering routine:

  • Water when the topsoil is dry.
  • Reduce watering in winter
  • Use pots with drainage holes.

Additionally, never leave trays with lots of water because it causes fungal issues. The healthy watering results in the accelerated growth of leaves and increased length of vines.

Does fertilizer really help money plants grow faster?

Yes, but only when it is used right. Excessive fertilizer may harm roots.

Best fertilizer tips:

  • Apply liquid organic fertilizer after every 15 days.
  • Once a month is sufficient for vermicompost.
  • Do not use chemical fertilizers indoors.

Moreover, the dilute compost tea is very effective in leaf shine and length of vines.

How to prune a money plant for faster growth?

Pruning stimulates new branches and complete growth. Even though this step is often overlooked by people, it is quite significant.

Simple pruning method:

  • Cut just above a leaf node
  • Remove yellow or weak leaves.
  • Use clean scissors.

Therefore, the energy goes into new growth rather than weak vines. You can also grow new plants using cuttings, especially if you buy healthy online indoor plants in Indore.

Best indoor placement for healthy growth

Placement influences light, airflow, and growth direction.

Best indoor spots:

  • Close to east or north windows.
  • On shelves that are indirectly lit.
  • Suspension of baskets along the balconies.

Additionally, do not keep money plants close to AC vents or heaters because dry air retards growth.

Common mistakes that slow down growth

Numerous indoor money plants develop at a slow pace because of simple errors.

Avoid these errors:

  • Having a plant in full shade.
  • Overwatering daily
  • Using heavy clay soil
  • Never pruning

However, these minor errors can be corrected to enhance development in a few weeks.

Conclusion

It is not difficult to grow a money plant faster inside the house when the fundamentals are followed correctly. It makes a great difference whether there is good light, balanced watering, proper soil, and timely pruning.
