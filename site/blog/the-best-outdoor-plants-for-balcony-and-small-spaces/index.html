Why choose outdoor plants for small spaces?

Outdoor plants are ideal for balconies and small spaces. They bring nature inside your home. Besides, they can purify the air and reduce stress. Most of the small plants are ideal for small spaces. You don’t need a garden to garden. Moreover, garden plants in the outside world serve as dwellings for butterflies and bees, promoting biodiversity. Succulents, herbs, and climbers take up minimal space optimally and are maintenance-free. They thrive well and grow magnificently. Therefore, even beginners can try out balcony gardening. It is an extremely fulfilling hobby appropriate for city lifestyles.

Key tips for growing plants on balconies

Balcony gardening is a wonderful hobby but does involve some planning. Here are some tips that would prove useful:

  • Check sunlight: Determine the amount of sunlight your balcony gets daily. This enables you to choose plants that are best suited for your light conditions.
  • Select the right plants: Pick plants that do well in your balcony condition. Some need full sun, but others need partial shade.
  • Utilize smart pots: Choose space-saving pots that also accommodate the growing of roots and breathing.
  • Water judiciously: Make the soil moist but not saturated. Too much water damages plant roots.
  • Utilize decent soil: Mulch or fertilize with organic fertilizer in good-draining, fertile soil to ensure healthy growth.
  • Don’t overcrowd: Don’t place plants too close together. Leave space for airflow.
  • Experiment with vertical gardening: use hanging pots, racks, or wall planters and save space and cultivate more.
  • Clean regularly: Dust and check leaves for diseases or pest infestation.

Best outdoor plants for balcony and small spaces

Choose plants according to space, sunlight, and your preference. Some of the best options that perform well in limited outdoor spaces are listed below.

  • Succulents and Cacti

Succulents are preferred in confined spaces. They possess thick leaves in which they retain water. So, they need less water. Cacti are low maintenance as well and come in many shapes. They are resistant to heat and are suitable for balcony use during summer. You can plant many different succulents in tiny pots together. They add unique color and texture. Succulents grow well in bright and half-shaded areas as well.

  • Flowering Plants

Flowering plants add color and fragrance to balconies. Petunias, geraniums, and marigolds are perfect choices. They bloom abundantly in small pots and window boxes. The blooms are a source of attraction for bees and butterflies. Besides, they fill the space with vibrant colors. You can also change flowering plants seasonally so that they bloom throughout the year. Ensure regular watering, especially if it is hot outside.

  • Herbs and Edibles

Herb growing is useful and fun. Mint, basil, and coriander are all suitable to be grown in pots. Fresh herbs can be used for cooking. In addition, herbs give a lovely fragrance to your balcony. Cherry tomatoes, which are some small vegetable plants, also suit the space. Herbs require moderate sunshine and watering. They develop fast and can be picked often.

  • Climbers and Trailing Plants

Climbers occupy less space by growing in the upwards direction. Money plant, ivy, and jasmine are good choices. They can be supported by trellises or balcony railings. Plants like strings of pearls have a beautiful cascading effect. These plants give green cover without taking up floor space. Furthermore, they help in providing balcony privacy.

How to care for balcony plants in summer?

Summer is tough for balcony plants due to heat. However, some plants can stand summer heat better than others. The best outdoor plants for summer in Indore withstand strong sun. Water your plants during early morning or late evening. This prevents quick evaporation of water. Use mulch on the soil surface to save water. Additionally, provide shade during strong sun hours if need be. Check for pests regularly and cut away rotting leaves. Plant nourishment with light fertilizer promotes healthy growth. Following these tips keeps your plants fresh during summer.
