Why choose summer flowering plants?

Summer-blooming flowers possess unique attributes. They withstand heat and sun more than other flowers. Furthermore, they make your garden active when most plants lose their vibrancy. They flower in their beautiful colors, and bees and butterflies visit them. This sustains the surrounding ecosystem. 

Additionally, summer flowering plants enhance the air quality surrounding your home. They also provide a cooling effect in hot weather. Thus, cultivating these plants is good for your garden and health as well.

Best flowering plants for summer in India

The best flowering plants for summer in Indore or India need to be known. These plants should endure the heat and yet come into bloom with beauty. 

Popular Summer Flowering Plants

  • Hibiscus: Large, showy flowers. It flowers all summer.
  • Bougainvillea: Tough climber with pink or orange flowers.
  • Marigold: Simple to grow with golden-yellow flowers.
  • Sunflower: Tall plants with large yellow flowers that move their faces to follow the sun.
  • Zinnia: Beautiful and water-resistant, ideal for Indian summers.
  • Periwinkle (Vinca): Flowers heavily with pink, white, or purple flowers.

Caring for summer flowering plants

Care for summer flowering plants is essential. Even heat-resistant plants require care. Adhere to these guidelines to keep your plants healthy:

  • Water plants in the morning or late evening.
  • Avoid overwatering to avoid root rot.
  • Mulch helps maintain soil moisture.
  • Remove dead flowers to promote new blooms.
  • Shade plants from the intense afternoon sun using shade nets.
  • Fertilize with organic manure to build up the health of plants.

By doing this, your plants will flower throughout the summer. These will work even if you purchase from an online plant nursery in Indore or a neighborhood shop.

Best indoor plants for summer in India

Indoor plants are a great way to stay cool at home. Many plants thrive indoors in summer, purifying the air and adding beauty.

Recommended Indoor Plants for Indore Summer

  • Spider Plant: Easy to maintain and improves air quality.

  • Snake Plant: Tolerates low water and indirect light well.

However, indoor plants need fewer sun rays directly on them but should be watered frequently. You can buy the best indoor plants for summer in Indore from local nurseries or websites.

Tips to keep your plants healthy during summer

Summer requires additional care for your plants. Some essential tips follow:

  • Water plants with plenty of water but not as often.
  • Use organic mulch to retain moisture.
  • Utilize natural insecticides such as neem oil.
  • Don’t fertilize during very hot weather.
  • Shift potted plants to the shade in the hottest afternoon sun.
  • Monitor regularly for pests and diseases.

These little things can keep your plants growing and flowering all summer.
