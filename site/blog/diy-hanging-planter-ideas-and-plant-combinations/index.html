DIY Hanging Planter Ideas and Plant Combinations

One of the simplest methods of making a home look fresher without incurring a lot of expense is hanging planters. They conserve floor area, introduce greenery at eye level, and give any space a warm and vibrant appearance. Most individuals go to the best plant nursery in Indore to get new plants to decorate their homes, whereas DIY planters enable you to be more creative and customize your area. Additionally, you may have a balcony, a corner to a window, or even a small room and create hanging planters, which will appear beautiful and feel special.

Why are hanging planters becoming popular?

Hanging planters provide that natural appearance that people desire in their homes, and they do not consume additional space. They are appropriate in small rooms, balconies, offices, and even kitchens and bring a touch of art. Spaces are serene with their soft motion and sunshine. Besides, they can be made using simple materials and are a popular option when it comes to customized greenery.

Simple DIY hanging planter ideas

The following are some of the ideas that do not need high skills. You can make them using things that you already have at home.

  1. Rope and pot hanging planter

This is the oldest and least advanced concept.

  • Take a clay or plastic pot.
  • Tie it with a heavy jute rope.
  • Make several knots at the same distance.
  • Hang it from a ceiling hook.

Therefore, it provides a natural appearance that is very warm, particularly when there are trailing plants. Moreover, you are also able to paint the pot in gentle colours that suit your room.

  1. Recycled glass jar planter

The old glass jars are used to create beautiful hanging planters.

  • Clean the jar well.
  • Insert small stones as drainage.
  • Place your plant inside.
  • Fasten the top using a heavy rope or metal.

Although jars are simple, they reflect the sunlight and ensure that your plants stand out. They do not work effectively in dark areas without a lot of light that is close to windows or balconies.

  1. Wooden frame hanging box

This is an effective one in case you like a rustic style.

  • Prepare a small wooden box or make one with the help of the leftover wooden pieces.
  • Hook heavy ropes to four sides.
  • Place two small pots inside.
  • Hang it a bit down so as to have a layered effect.

This design is very beautiful in the balconies and in the open spaces. Additionally, it also sustains the bigger plants since the foundation is firm.

  1. Hanging coconut shell planter

Coconut shells are natural and green.

  • Cut the shell in half.
  • Wipe it and make small holes at the sides.
  • Take three ropes and bring them to the top.
  • Add soil and plant a small trailing.

The shells of the coconuts provide a tropical impression and keep the vegetation cool. Moreover, they should be kept in the shade since they dry quicker.

  1. Hanging planter of metal basket

When you have old metal baskets, use them instead of disposing of them.

  • Cover the basket with a piece of coco peat or cloth.
  • Add dirt and plant your greenery.
  • Hang using a chain or rope.

Metal baskets are strong and ideal in complete plant decorations. Additionally, you may combine various types of plants to achieve a green appearance.

Best plant combinations for hanging displays

It is significant to select the appropriate plants. Not every plant can be planted in hanging planters. Therefore, consider light, humidity, and size of plants when combining varieties.

Some simple and beautiful combinations are listed below:

  1. Trailers + Bushy Plants

An ideal harmony of flowing and full shapes.

Suggested Mix:

  • Money plant (trailed down)
  • Syngonium (compact and leafy)

The long stems fall gracefully, and the bushy plant fills the center.

  1. Bright spaces colourful mix

Good on balconies that receive much sun.

Suggested Mix:

  • Spider plant
  • Inch plant
  • Green wandering vine

However, this provides a beautiful contrast of stripes, purple color, and bright green leaves.

  1. Soft hanging greens of corners calm

Best in bedrooms and study rooms.

Suggested Mix:

  • Fern
  • Baby tears plant

However, ferns are voluminous, and baby tears are diffused to produce a calming effect.

  1. Succulent hanging bowl

Succulents are not very demanding but require light.

Suggested Mix:

  • String of pearls
  • Small rosette succulents

Moreover, this mix is stylish and consumes minimal water.

  1. Kitchen hanging herbs

Good for everyday use and smells fresh.

Suggested Mix:

  • Mint
  • Basil
  • Thyme

These are herbs that grow well and can be easily picked during cooking. Besides, they give your kitchen a fresh and vibrant smell.

  1. Balcony fancy mix

To those who prefer a more elaborate appearance.

Suggested Mix:

  • Petunias
  • Lobelia
  • Creeping Jenny

The colours are well blended as they grow and dangle. Moreover, they require a lot of attention, but they pay you with flowers.

Tips to maintain your hanging planters

Even the most successful DIY planter requires some attention. The following are easy ways to maintain healthy plants:

  • Water gradually to ensure that the soil takes it in.
  • A light soil mixture is used to ensure that the planter is not too heavy.
  • Before selecting plants, check if sunlight is required.
  • Turn the planter every week to ensure that every side receives equal light.
  • Eliminate dry leaves to ensure that plants are clean.
  • Use a small plate under pots indoors to avoid dripping

Frequently Asked Questions

  1. Why are DIY hanging planters a good option in small houses?

They do not occupy much space on the floor, introduce greenery to eye level, and provide a new stylish appearance with ease.

  1. What are the best plants to grow using simple DIY hanging planters?

Plants, ferns, herbs, and small succulents are growing well with adequate light and frequent attention.

  1. How can beginners maintain healthy hanging planters at home?

Water carefully, use lightweight soil, ensure good sunlight, rotate planters, and trim dry leaves regularly.
