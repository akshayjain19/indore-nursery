Understanding semi-indoor plants

Semi-indoor plants are plants which are partial sunlight or indirect light tolerant. However, they can be employed to enhance the look of areas such as balconies, verandas, shaded windows, and partially open corridors.

Most Vital Characteristics of Semi-Indoor Plants:

  • Withstand minimal direct sun exposure.
  • Do not complain about moderate temperature changes.
  • Requires moderate care and watering.

Additionally, some of the most common ones include areca palm, snake plant, money plant, and peace lily.

The connection between vastu and plants

Vastu Shastra is all about the movement of positive energy in a space. Ancient science comes to show us that some plants can influence prosperity, relationships, and health.

How the plants keep Vastu balance:

  • Flow of energy: Positive plants repel negative vibrations and bring in positive energy.
  • Directional placement: For example, having a Tulsi plant in the northeast direction is said to bring spiritual gains.

Therefore , selecting the appropriate semi-indoor plants and planting them according to Vastu can balance your house with the Vastu principles too.

Placement tips for vastu benefits

Correct placement is the most crucial factor in getting the maximum benefits out of your semi-indoor plants.

  • Northeast corner: Ideal for plants which are religious in nature, like Tulsi.
  • Southeast corner: Ideal place for money plants to bring money and wealth.

  • Living room: Have air-purifying plants installed to become healthier and happier.

However , do not maintain the prickly plants (except for roses) indoors because they cause relationship tension.

Caring for semi-indoor plants

So that you can have the most benefit of your plants, you must give them good care so that they will remain healthy and look pretty for a long time.

  • Water them when the surface soil is dry. Water only after checking the soil with your hands. Too much watering will rot the roots and the plant.
  • Provide them with indirect sunlight for a few hours a day. Set them in front of a sunny window where the sun’s light is not direct but soft.
  • Occasionally wash leaves so that they may breathe easier. Sprinkle with a soft object or a gentle water spray.
  • Repot them periodically, two or three times annually, for average growth. This gives more room to their roots and new soil nutrients.

Furthermore, if you too are time-constrained, opt for online indoor plants in Indore for low-maintenance plants. They are not high on maintenance but give the beauty and freshness of the plants to the surroundings too.

Common mistakes to avoid

A majority of individuals do not get the hoped-for benefits of Vastu plants due to some common and avoidable errors:

  • Overwatering, causing root rot. The plant requires water, but excessive water causes the roots to die and the roots to rot and kill the plant. Check the soil before watering at all times.
  • Shading plants in dark, poorly ventilated areas. Plants will not grow as well with insufficient light and fresh air. They wilt and lose their bright color.
  • Small pots that restrict the growth of roots. When roots do not receive space to expand, the plant comes to a standstill and falls ill in the long run.
  • Not paying much attention to insects until they breed. A tiny insect will chew leaf and stem if not treated promptly, thus weakening the plant.

However, by carrying out all this, you will get your plants healthy, hence spreading health and Vastu benefits.
