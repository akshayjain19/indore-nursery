Low-Cost Seasonal Plants to Refresh Your Garden Every Year

2025-03-03

Gardening is a fulfilling hobby that brings elegance and beauty to any house. A healthy and colourful garden not only beautifies the outdoor area but also provides a serene atmosphere. However, it can be costly to maintain a garden if you opt for flowers that are hard to maintain. To maintain your garden in an amazing state without breaking the bank, seasonal flowers are the way to go.

These plants are simple to grow in particular seasons, simple to replace, and affordable. As a new gardener or an experienced gardener, seasonal plants will assist you in bringing life to your garden with little effort. Below are the advantages of seasonal plants and some affordable choices for every season.

Why Choose Seasonal Plants?

Seasonal plants have several benefits compared to permanent plants. They are easy to modify to suit seasonal weather changes and are less costly to maintain. Some of the main reasons why one should choose seasonal plants include:

  • Cost-Effective: Seasonal plants cost less than perennial plants.
  • Quick Growth: Seasonal plants develop quickly and are very attractive for their duration.
  • Plenty of Choices: Various seasons have various flowers and plants, and your garden will always look fresh.
  • Low Maintenance: Seasonal plants are not very demanding and, hence, great for beginners.

Furthermore, seasonal plants are readily available everywhere. Be it visiting the best plant nursery in Indore or buying online from an Indore online plant nursery, there is an ample selection of seasonal plants in Indore for a reasonable price.

Affordable Summer Plants

Summer is ruthless on plants because of the dryness and heat. Still, some plants make it through the scorching summer very well and need very little care. Some of the cheap ones are:

  1. Marigolds

Marigolds are among the summer flowers that are most commonly sought. Marigolds are available in yellow, orange, and red varieties. Marigolds are very easy to grow and repel insects as well, making them great to plant in vegetable gardens.

  1. Zinnias

Zinnias are vibrant, rapidly growing flowers that bring any garden to life. They are easy to maintain and flower all summer long. What’s more, they are attractors of butterflies, which create nature’s own beauty in your landscape.

  1. Portulaca (Moss Rose)

Portulaca or moss rose is also resistant to drought and heat. It bears coloured flowers that open even in the scorching heat. It is an easy-care flower that suits best for those with no time for plant care.

These summer flowers are easily accessible at the largest nursery in Indore and nearby local nurseries.

Budget-Friendly Winter Flowers

Winter gardens are lovely when adorned with the appropriate seasonal flowers. Winter is the perfect time for certain flowers to bloom, providing your garden with an injection of colours and vibrancy. Some budget-friendly winter flowers include:

  1. Pansies

Pansies are winter flowers that add a splash of colour to your garden. They are cold-resistant and low-maintenance.

  1. Calendula

Calendula, or pot marigold, is a vibrant winter flower. It is medicinal and can be utilized for skin care and herbal tea.

  1. Snapdragons

Snapdragons are thin, tall flowers that flower during the winter season. They are multi colored and provide height to garden borders.

Local gardens or an Indore online nursery plant shop can supply winter plants.

Monsoon Plants That Grow Easily

The monsoon season offers sufficient water, which causes some plants to grow faster. Some low-cost monsoon plants are:

  1. Balsam

Balsam plants are simple to cultivate and bear flowers of every color. Balsam plants like moist soil and don’t need much care.

  1. Coleus

Coleus is a colorful leafy plant that prefers to flower during the rainy season. Its colorful leaves are simply ideal for any garden.

  1. Ginger Lily

Ginger lilies are scented flowers that prefer to be moist. They bring a splash of tropicality and freshness to your garden.

All these monsoon plants are available at Indore’s largest nursery at affordable prices.

How to Care for Seasonal Plants?

Seasonal plant care is easy if you do it the right way. Here are some important tips:

  1. Choose the Right Soil

Good soil nourishes plants and makes them healthy.

  1. Watering

Overwatering can kill plants. Water as needed based on the season and plant.

  1. Give Adequate Sunlight

Position plants to get the right amount of sunlight. Some plants require full sunlight, whereas others require shade.

  1. Offer Organic Fertilizers

Compost and organic fertilizers cause plants to be healthy without chemicals.

Be it buying from a local shop or an online plant nursery in Indore, using the above tips, your plants will be fresh and healthy.

Conclusion

Seasonal flowers are a great means of achieving a yearly make-over on your garden without overspending. They are inexpensive, low maintenance, and provide seasonal colour to your garden. Summer has marigolds and zinnias, winter has pansies and snapdragons, and each season in between provides a choice.

Whether you go to the  biggest nursery in Indore or shop online, you will be able to get plenty of economical choices. Plant and have a green garden all year long!
