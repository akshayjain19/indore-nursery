Creative DIY Hanging Plant Holders for Your Home

2025-03-01

Plants bring a little elegance and freshness to any home. They purify the air and make rooms beautiful. Nevertheless, having plants in pots on the floor or shelves takes up too much space. That’s why hanging plant containers are just perfect. They bring greenery without filling your home with too much furniture and a style point.

If you are a serious DIY enthusiast, creating your own hanging plant holders can be a rewarding and entertaining experience. You can design them according to your preferences and home decor. Let’s have a look at some simple and creative DIY ideas that will turn your indoor area into a greener and more attractive space.

Why Hanging Plant Holders?

Plant hanging plant holders are ideal for homes with limited space. They enable you to hang plants above the ground and possess a decorative feature for your indoor spaces. Moreover, they safeguard plants from pets or small children who may accidentally destroy them. You can place them in front of windows, in corners, or even suspended from the ceiling to create a marvellous visual experience.

If you would like to pick a range of plants to put in your new holders, visit an online Indore plant nursery. They possess a good amount of indoor plants that are not difficult to keep and flower beautifully.

DIY Hanging Plant Holder Ideas

There are several DIY ways of making your own plant pots. You can have a contemporary, country, or bohemian look depending on your preference. There is a design to suit everyone. Here are some easy DIY ideas to help you along.

  1. Macramé Plant Hangers

Macramé plant hangers add a boho-chic touch to any setting. They are created from strong cotton rope and several basic knots. Additionally, they are fashionable and suitable for any room. You can try various designs and shades to coordinate with your décor.

  1. Hanging Basket Planters

Woven baskets are great to plant holders. They provide a natural, country look while providing good air circulation for roots. They also retain moisture, which is perfect for plants that require humid environments. Just add a plastic liner within the basket to avoid leaks.

  1. Repurposed Tin Can Holders

Upcycling tin cans into plant pots is an excellent way of upcycling household items. They can be coloured with vibrant hues, covered with decorative cloth, or covered with rope for a fresh appearance. Additionally, they are light and easy to hang with a few holes drilled into them.

For more plant varieties to use for your DIY works, check out a plant nursery in Indore. They have excellent collections to hang indoors and outdoors.

  1. Wooden Shelf Hanging Planters

A wooden rack can be transformed into a beautiful hanging plant stand with creative thinking. You simply have to mount ropes on both sides of a hardwood plank and suspend it from the ceiling. Furthermore, the process also forces you to load more plants onto one rack, providing your home with an elegant green alcove.

  1. Glass Bottle Planters

Reuse glass bottles. Slice them, fill them with soil, and place tiny indoor greens there. Create a rope to wrap around the neck and hang them for an understated charm.

  1. Wire Plant Holders

Wire holders are also a popular and contemporary choice to suspend plants. You can mould metal wires into shapes using geometric designs to support plant pots. Additionally, they are very durable and complement different interior decors.

Top Plants for Hanging Holders

Not every plant does well in hanging planters, so it’s a good idea to pick the right ones. Here are some top recommendations:

  • Pothos: A low-maintenance plant that does well in low light.
  • Spider Plant: A low-maintenance air-purifying plant.
  • Ferns: Ideal for steamy spaces such as bathrooms.
  • String of Pearls: A stunning trailing succulent that is ideal for hangers.

These are easily available at an Indore online plant nursery. They infuse natural beauty into any room with little effort.

Where to Hang Your Plants?

The placement of your hanging plants is important so that they can grow nicely. Here are some suggestions:

  • Hang them near windows so that they get indirect sunlight.
  • Put them in the living room to create a snug, green ambience.
  • Install ceiling hooks in patios or balconies for a feel of the outdoors.
  • Avoid keeping them near air conditioners or heaters.

For additional inspiration, go to a plant nursery in Indore. They offer advice on selecting and positioning plants in your home.

How to Take Care of Hanging Plants?

The care of hanging plants is a little different from pot plants. They dry quicker being higher up. This is how you can keep them:

  • Watering: Avoid overwatering. Allow the soil to dry out a bit before watering again.
  • Trimming: Remove dead leaves from time to time to have the plant look fresh.
  • Sunlight: Set them in sunlight but not under direct, intense sunlight.
  • Fertilizing: Fertilize your plants once a month to encourage growth.
