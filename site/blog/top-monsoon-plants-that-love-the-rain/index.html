Monsoon season is the time when gardens all around erupt with new life. The rain ensures that several plants become strong and healthy. There are plants that absolutely adore the monsoon. They prosper in heavy rains and green surroundings. Growing best monsoon plants may bring you freshness and beauty to your home space. 

This blog will take you through the most effective monsoon plants that adore rain. Furthermore, you will know how to take care of them and where to purchase them in Indore. Even with large or small gardens or even tiny balconies, monsoon plants can lighten up your area.

Why do some plants love the monsoon?

Monsoon plants prefer moist and wet positions. They admire the rain and soak water and nutrients quickly from the soil. Rainwater also removes dust and dirt from their leaves. This keeps them fresh and healthy. Too much water isn’t liked by all plants, though. Some plants are hurt by root rot if the soil is too wet. 

However, extra moisture can also cause plant diseases. That is why choosing the right plants during monsoons is required. Monsoon plants are strong and will avoid heavy rains and even small floods. They are growing quickly and will make your garden beat and lively. Therefore, if you choose the right people, your monsoon garden will be cute.

Best monsoon plants to grow at home

Here are some of the well-known plants that thrive well during monsoons. They are easy to maintain and will adorn your garden.

  • Marigold (Tagetes)

Marigolds flower nicely in monsoons. They prefer soil that is well-drained. Its yellow and orange flowers are butterfly attractors.

  • Hibiscus

Hibiscus prefers humid climatic conditions and recurrent rains. It has big, colorful flowers. These flowers are air purifiers too.

  • Money Plant (Epipremnum aureum)

This is very resistant and survives in moist soil. It can grow indoors and outdoors. Besides this, it is considered a bringer of good luck.

  • Lotus

Lotus plants live in water bodies such as ponds and lakes. They bloom beautifully in the rainy season. They represent purity and beauty.

  • Fern

Ferns survive in damp and dark places. Their beautiful green fronds look lovely during the monsoon.

  • Bamboo

Bamboo grows very quickly in rain. It is a hardy plant and a good choice for decoration and fencing.

  • Taro (Colocasia)

Taro likes waterlogged soil. Its huge leaves add a tropical touch to your garden landscape. It prospers under heavy rains.

  • Ginger and Turmeric

They are root vegetables that require waterlogged soil. Monsoon facilitates proper development of their roots. They are great kitchen spices too.

  • Jasmine

Jasmine blooms wonderfully well during monsoons. Their lovely fragrance fills the air and makes it sweet-smelling and your garden fascinating.

How to care for monsoon plants?

Maintenance of these plants involves water and soil care. Here are some hints:

  • Plant them in well-draining soil so that they don’t waterlog.
  • Don’t overwater; nature’s rain would take care of it.
  • Remove dead leaves to prevent fungal growth.
  • Give partial shadows to plants that do not like sunbeams.
  • The country uses organic materials so that the soil is moist, but there is no waterfall.
  • Check the plants often for pests that flourish under humid conditions.
  • Maintain climbers with sticks or trailis.

Additionally, these plants develop more rapidly; therefore, regularly prune them for shaping. Healthy growth and colorful flowers are guaranteed with care.
