Top indoor plants that thrive in low light

Selecting the right plant is the secret to success. Here are some of the best plants that can tolerate low light:

  • Snake Plant (Sansevieria)

It tolerates very low light and minimal water. It is also an efficient air cleaner.

  • ZZ Plant (Zamioculcas zamiifolia)

The ZZ plant contains waxy leaves that are shadow-tolerant. It is slow growing but stable.

  • Pothos (Devils Ivy)

Pothos is a climbing plant with heart shaped leaves. It performs low light and roots well.

  • Peace Lily (Spathiphyllum)

Peace lilies flower under low light. Peace lilies are also air cleaners.

  • Philodendron

It is liked for having beautiful green leaves.It grows indoors in low light.

  • Cast Iron Plant (Aspidistra elatior)

As the name itself says, it is very rigid. It grows well in shaded and abandoned areas.

  • Chinese evergreen (Aglaonema)

It is a diverse variety that grows well in low light. Leaves may be green, silver, or variegated.

They are ideal plants for home decor in Indore. They provide greenness without insisting on direct sunlight.

How to care for low light indoor plants?

While low-light plants are simple to grow, they require some aspects to be taken care of. Mentioned below are some easy tips to keep them healthy and cheerful:

  • Give your plants only water when the topsoil is dry to touch. Excess water can rotate the roots, decay the roots and brown them. Do not give them too much water at a time.
  • The leaves become dusty and get less sunlight. Clean the leaves with soft, damp cloth and again. It breathes your plant and remains healthy.
  • To give your plant some light on both sides, turn the pot every week. This makes your plant grow evenly.
  • Use pots with drainage holes and a potting mixture before it will waterlog. Feeding your plants lightly in spring and summer so that they grow strongly without overfitting. 
  • Finally, avoid placing plants where cold drafts are present or are in front of the air conditioner because rapid temperature rapid rapid stress gives them stress.

Remember that every plant is different. When buying from a reliable source like the Indore online indoor plant vendors, they usually include care tips so your plants can thrive.

Indoor plants are not just for decoration. Here are some of the benefits:

  • Beauty Value

Plants bring color, texture, and vitality to a space.

  • Noise Abatement

Plants dampen sound, which quiets areas.

Research indicates that plants improve concentration and creativity.
