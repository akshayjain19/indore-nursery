5 indoor plants to buy this summer for home and offices

Indoor plants or household plants brighten our indoor space and make feel us fresh, calm, and happy. From shade-tolerant indoor plants to plants that tolerate sunlight, we have plants for every corner of our home and office. 

It is the start of March month and winter almost passed out now we feel some warmness in the weather which indicates summer is coming. When we hear summer we already think about high temperatures, sunburn, and suffocation, and for prevention, we look towards fresh and cold air. The importance of plants and trees we actually realize in the summer season when we try to find a peaceful shelter under a tree. In this blog, Indore Nursery presents the 5 best indoor plants which you need to buy this summer season for your home and office.

Peace Lily –

Peace Lily in Hindi is known as शांति का पौधा. And its scientific Name is Spathiphyllum, and Peace lily also known as Cobra Plant.

The peace lily plant is a symbol of calmness, freshness, Purity, Innocence, Peace, and Sympathy. It is the best plant to gift your friends, family, and loved ones.

This plant is planted in homes to purify the air. It comes in the list of the most popular house plants. Many species are found all over the world.

And it is an evergreen plant that purifies the air and makes you feel fresh that’s why this is our first choice as the best plant for the summer season

Normally we people forget to water our indoor plants, or we have a question mark when we have to water peace lily. So there is one very helpful feature of the Peace Lily (for people like you who don’t know when to give water to your Peace Lily) is that it will hang or lie down when it needs watering; it will Hang down and ask for water. After you water it, it will be fine in the future. This is hardly an ideal way to treat your plants, but this one is very forgiving that way. Keep your Peace Lily Plants in a prominent spot so you notice them consistently.

Snack plant –

The snack plant’s scientific name is Dracaena Trifasciata, and it is also known as Mother-in-law’s tongue, and Snack plant’s Hindi name is “सास की जीभ”. 

These days there is a lot of trend for indoor plants. People like to have indoor plants in their homes or office. This not only increases the beauty of the house but also brings positive energy along with it. Apart from this, trees and plants also have a lot of importance in Vastu Shastra. Some such plants have been mentioned in Vastu Shastra, planting them inside the house brings happiness and positive energy. It s one of these plants. According to Vastu, snake plants must be planted in the house.

Snack plant is counted among air-purifying plants. This plant also helps in bringing mental peace by reducing your stress.

If you keep this plant in the bedroom or living room, it brings happiness and prosperity. However, one thing should be kept in mind keep it in such a place in the living room where the guests coming to the house can see it.

Bonsai Indoor Plants –

The scientific name of Bonsai Plat is Pomegranate Dwarf Punica Granatum Nana. Bonsai is a Japanese word that means Shallow Pan Plant ( Bon – Shallow Pan, Sai – Plant ).

You already know that bonsai plants enhance the decor in your home, terrace, or office. But there is much more to a bonsai plant. Bonsai plants benefit the psyche of the owner or gardener. Bonsai plants require a lot of care and time. You will learn to love and care. You will also practice patience. Develop the traits of consistency, confidence, self-control, and self-awareness. Explore your creative side as you style bonsai plants in their splendid form. If you have a bonsai tree inside your house, it releases oxygen and purifies the air inside your house. Placing bonsai plants outside has similar benefits as well as providing a green area to de-stress or meditate. A bonsai tree can reduce the frequency of indoor dry skin, sore throat, and cold as they retain moisture in the atmosphere. They are great gifting options. Finally, who needs a lifelong friend? grow a bonsai. 

 

Lucky Bamboo –

Dracaena Sanderiana is the Scientific name of Lucky Bamboo.

Lucky Bamboo is an easy-to-care-for indoor plant that grows in low to indirect sunlight. In fact, it is not bamboo but a tropical water lily from Africa called Dracaena Sanderiana. It is said that in the place where it grows, everyone gets happiness and good fortune.

According to Feng Shui, keeping a bamboo plant in the house brings prosperity and happiness. It is believed that the people of the house where this plant is grown do not have health-related problems.

Money Plant –

The scientific name of the Money Plant is Epipremnum aureum. Money Plant is one of the most popular plants to be planted at home. People like to plant the vine of this plant inside their houses and office. 

It is also believed that if a money plant is planted in the house, it brings happiness, peace, and prosperity to the house.
