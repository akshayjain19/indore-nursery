Introduction

Plants bring life, color and freshness to a room. However, not all rooms get enough sunlight. There are many houses, workplaces and apartments, which have corners that stay dark throughout the day. The good news is that you can also cultivate healthy, beautiful plants at such a place. 

Moreover, all you have to do is choose those who naturally thrive in low light. These plants are easy to take care of and also helps in purifying the air and makes your location feel calm and welcome. In this blog, we will explain some most suitable indoor plants for low light places. With the right choice, even the dullest corner can turn into a vibrant, green area.

Why choose low-light indoor plants?

Not every area of your house gets sunlight. Bathrooms, corridors and small rooms do not get any sunlight. Nevertheless, you can keep beautiful plants in them using plants that grow under low light. The plants are shade-flexible and do not require much care. Additionally, they use less water and make less demand than plants growing under sunlight, so they are perfect for a start. 

However, if your life is very busy, then these are the right plants or choose from an online plant nursery in indore, because they also survive with minimal maintenance. If you choose some of them carefully, you can bring new leaves and greenery to any dark corner of the house without worrying about high maintenance care.

Key benefits of low-light indoor plants

  • Air Support: Most  indoor plants purify the air by eliminating toxins.
  • Relief from stress: Plants reduce stress and improve mood.
  • Space friendly: They do not require large utensils or gardens.
  • Low maintenance: They require less water and maintenance than external plants.

Top indoor plants that thrive in low light

Here are some well -liked plants that are like shady environment:

Snake plant

The snake plant is the easiest plant to care. It likes to be neglected. It can thrive in very low light and only water has to be obtained when the soil is dried. This indoor air plant also purifies indoor air, so indoor air is healthier.

Zz plant (zamioculcas zamiifolia)

The ZZ plant is highly tolerant of dark surroundings. It contains bright green leaves that are highly decorative. Therefore, the ZZ plant is capable of storing water in the roots and hence it does not require continuous water. ZZ plant is an ideal choice for low -light offices or homes.

Peace Lily

Peace Lily is another low-light flower with beautiful white flowers. It thrives in dark rooms and adds a refreshing appearance to all rooms. On top of that, it purifies air by eliminating formaldehyde.

Pothos (Money Plant)

Pothos is a favorite indoor plant due to the ease with which it grows. It will survive in extremely low light. You can grow it in water or soil. Its vines spill beautifully and would be a nice choice for hanging baskets or shelving.

Spider Plant

Spider Plant prefers indirect light and can do well in a variety of conditions. Additionally, it also produces baby plants, or “pups,” that can be easily replanted. This plant is also very famous for air purification.

Philodendron

Philodendrons bloom in dark areas and require regular care. They are given heart-shaped leaves, and as such, they are ideal for homes. They function well in small containers and appear marvelous on the table top.

Cast Iron Plant

As its name implies, the Cast Iron Plant is a tough and robust plant. It can tolerate minimal light, dust, and even neglect. The plant must be planted in dark spaces where other plants perish.

Tips to care for low-light plants

Do not overwater; the majority of plants like soil to be dry in between waterings.

  • Use well-draining soil to avoid root rot.
  • Clean leave regularly to avoid waste buildup.
  • Turn plants every few weeks to ensure balanced growth.
  • Fertilize monthly when the plant is growing.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Placing plants in totally dark rooms (they still require some light).
  • Overwatering, which causes fungal disease.
  • Using the incorrect pot size, which can hinder growth.
  • Forgetting pest attacks such as mealybugs or aphids.

FAQs

Do low-light indoor plants develop more rapidly using artificial light?

They develop steadily on fluorescent or LED lights but not as rapidly as in sunlight.

How often do I water low-light indoor plants?

Most need watering only when top soil is dry to touch.

Are low-light indoor plants pet-friendly?

Spider plants are pet-friendly, but Peace Lilies and Pothos are poisonous to pets.

Can I keep these plants in rooms with no windows?

Yes, but having some indirect light exposure keeps them healthier.
