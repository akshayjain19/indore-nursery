Oregano is a simple herb to grow in the house. It requires sunlight, light watering, and well-drained soil. Even beginners can grow it in pots or gardens. What do you need to start?

Preparations Before planting oregano, ensure that you have the basics. Even though oregano is easy to cultivate, it is better prepared.

Basic Requirements:

  • A small pot with drainage holes.
  • Properly drained soil (mix garden soil + compost + sand)
  • Oregano seeds or a small plant
  • A sunny place (at least 5-6 hours of sunlight)
  • Watering can

Should you grow from seeds or cuttings?

This is one of the beginner questions.

1. Growing from Seeds

  • Takes more time
  • Needs patience
  • Germination may take 7–14 days.

2. Growing from Cuttings or Nursery Plants

  • Faster growth
  • Easier for beginners
  • More reliable results

However, when you are a beginner gardener, it is better to start with a small plant. At Indore Nursery, there are usually ready oregano plants that are vigorous and easy to keep.

How to plant oregano correctly?

Proper planting is extremely essential. Nevertheless, it is not complicated when you can follow the following steps.

Step-by-Step Planting Guide:

  • Add a ready soil mixture to the pot.
  • Make a small hole in the center.
  • The plant should be placed carefully.
  • Place soil on top of the cover and press.
  • Lightly water after planting.

Do not press the soil too hard. Oregano roots need airflow. Additionally, it is important to always make sure that water drains freely since oregano hates wet soil.

If you are not sure of the quality of soil, you can seek advice from the experts at the biggest nursery in Indore.

How often should you water oregano?

The greatest error that beginners commit is overwatering. Oregano likes moderately dry soil. Thus, only when the upper layer of soil is dry.

Simple Watering Rule:

  • Summer: 2–3 times per week
  • Winter: Once per week
  • Rainy season: Water when the soil is dry.

Furthermore, watering should be done after checking soil moisture. Excess water may lead to root rot.

Does oregano need sunlight?

Yes, sunlight is very essential.Oregano requires a minimum of 56 hours of direct sunshine per week. Hence, put it on a balcony, terrace, or sunny window.

If sunlight is less,

  • Growth becomes slow.
  • Leaves lose strong flavor.
  • The plant becomes weak.

Moreover, turn the pot every few days to ensure that each side gets the same amount of sunlight.

Aroma and taste are enhanced when exposed to healthy sunlight. This is the reason gardeners like planting oregano in open spaces as opposed to shaded ones.

How to care for oregano daily?

Oregano does not require intensive attention. Nevertheless, little things make the plant stronger.

Easy Care Tips:

  • Remove dry leaves regularly.
  • Lightly trim the plant after every few weeks.
  • Avoid chemical fertilizers.
  • Use compost once a month.

Moreover, the plant is pruned to make it bushier. When you cut the leaves at the top, other branches grow at the sides.

Beginners are confused with the care of plants. However, advice from senior growers at IndoreNursery assists in the development of confidence.

How to harvest oregano leaves?

The plant can be harvested once it is 4-5 inches tall.

How to Harvest Properly:

  • Cut leaves in the morning.
  • Use clean scissors.
  • Do not cut off more than a third of the plant.
  • Pick before flowering to be rich in flavor.

Additionally, frequent harvesting causes the plant to be full. So, there is no reason to be afraid of trimming it down.

Fresh leaves of oregano may be applied in the following:

  • Pasta
  • Pizza
  • Soups
  • Herbal tea

Another thing that you can do is to dry the leaves and keep them in an airtight container.

Common problems and easy solutions

Although oregano is not a difficult plant to cultivate, minor problems can be observed.

1. Yellow Leaves

  • Cause: Overwatering
  • Solution: Watering less and better drainage.

2. Slow Growth

  • Cause: Less sunlight
  • Solution: Change the location of the plant to a brighter area.

3. Weak Smell

Cause: Too much shade

Solution: Direct sunlight.

4. Fungal Issues

  • Cause: Wet soil
  • Solution: Water in between watering.

Moreover, the majority of issues can be fixed easily provided that you pay close attention to the plant.
