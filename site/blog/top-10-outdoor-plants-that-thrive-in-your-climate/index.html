Understanding your climate for outdoor plants

Your weather determines what plants thrive outside. They require too much sunlight, while others prefer to live in shade. Additionally, the growth varies with temperature and rainfall. For example, Indore has a tropical climate with hot summers and not with cold winters. Therefore, the selection of heat resistant and dried resistant plants is extremely important. Furthermore, knowing your climate zone prevents expensive gardening mistakes.

Benefits of choosing the right outdoor plants

Selecting plants that flourish in your environment has numerous benefits. To start with, the plants need less attention and water. Secondly, the plants are less susceptible to pests and diseases. That is, you incur less money and time. Thirdly, healthy plants give your space a good appearance and also improve the quality of air. Finally, successful plants give you and your family members a peaceful outdoor area.

Best 10 outdoor plants

Below are some plants that have been tried and proved to excel in warm weather like Indore’s. They are easy to care for and add beauty to any garden.

  1. Bougainvillea

Bougainvillea is a popular flower for warm areas. It flowers with vivid, colored blooms year-round. The plant tolerates drought and can grow in bad soil. Furthermore, it is butterfly- and bird-attracting, so your garden is more full-fledged in the food chain.

  1. Hibiscus

Hibiscus are large, showy red, pink, and yellow flowers. Hibiscus likes full sun and dry conditions. Hibiscus creates a tropical feel in your yard. It also attracts pollinators such as hummingbirds and bees.

  1. Marigold

Marigolds are robust flowers that flower exquisitely under warm weather conditions. Marigolds are easy to take care of and are inherently pest-deterrent. The flowers also deter pests away from the other plants in your garden. Marigolds also come in a mix of different shades of yellow and orange.

  1. Plumeria (Frangipani)

Plumeria has fragrant, waxy flowers. It prefers warm temperatures with lots of sunshine. Plumeria is perfect for a patio or along a border in the yard. It must be watered from time to time but can withstand dry spells.

  1. Desert Rose (Adenium)

Desert Rose is a heat- and sun-resistant succulent plant with fleshy stems and flowers with red or pink colors. It is extremely low maintenance, requiring less watering, and thrives when planted in garden beds or container gardens. The plant also has a value of beauty due to its interesting shape.

  1. Jasmine

Jasmine is yellow or white with fragrance. They need full sun to partial shade. Jasmine is a rapid-growing vine that can be cultivated on trellises or fences. It is also honey bee- and butterfly-attracting.

  1. Snake Plant (Sansevieria)

Snake plants are drought-resistant and long-lived. They can grow in low-water and low-light environments. This air plant is clean and looks fashionable in an outside pot. Snake plants are standing, and this makes them perfect for small spaces.

  1. Aloe Vera

Aloe vera is a medicinal succulent. It can thrive in sunny areas and dry soil. Aloe requires little water and care. Its thick leaves store water, and it is thus suitable for hot temperatures.

  1. Lantana

Lantana bears flowers of different colors in clusters. It withstands heat and dry spells. Lantana is butterfly- and bird-friendly. It is also the best for ground cover or hedges.

  1. Hibiscus Mutabilis

This hibiscus color shifts with age in the bloom. It can stand heat and full sun. It is a quick grower and will grow quite large. Hibiscus mutabilis adds drama and color shift to landscapes.

How to care for your outdoor plants?

Outdoor plant care depends on species and climate. Some easy care tips follow:

  • Water plants early morning or late evening.
  • Mulch to conserve soil moisture.
  • Fertilize seasonally using organic compost.
  • Prune dead and broken branches regularly.
  • Shield from extreme weather conditions if required.
  • Inspect plants for pest occurrence weekly.

By doing the above, your plants will develop healthily. Seasonal plants in Indore also sometimes need some special care during hot summers and monsoons.

Seasonal plants in Indore: what to expect?

Indore experiences hot summer and mild winter climatic conditions. Seasonal plants in Indore also develop according to these seasons. Seasonal plants in Indore bring variety and color all year round. However, they bring vibrancy to your garden by the arrival of changing seasons.
