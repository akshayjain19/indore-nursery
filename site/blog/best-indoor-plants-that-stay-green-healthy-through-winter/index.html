During winter, there is usually dry air, the sun is not as bright, and the indoor air is colder. Due to this fact, most houseplants become dull or fail to grow. Why do some indoor plants struggle in winter?

To select plants that are not vulnerable to the winter season, one must know why most of the plants fail during this season.

  • The problem of sunlight is reduced because of shorter days.
  • Due to heaters, indoor air becomes dry.
  • The process of overwatering occurs when the soil becomes dry.
  • Plants are sensitive to changes in temperature.

Moreover, winter is best suited to plants that can withstand low light, dry air, and indoor conditions. These plants do not require additional care, and they are green with minimal care.

Indoor plants that stay green during winter

There are also indoor plants that are inherently strong and can survive winter. Even when the weather is harsh, they still appear healthy.

  • Snake Plant

This plant is characterized by upright leaves and deep green colour. It can withstand low light and requires minimum water. Additionally, it assists in enhancing air quality.

  • ZZ Plant

This plant has thick and shiny leaves and can easily endure dry air since it is a water store. It is slow growing and remains green all through winter without any problems.

  • Pothos

Pothos is a common houseplant since it adapts within a short period. The growth might be retarded during winter, but the leaves are green and fresh. It also appears good in hanging pots.

  • Peace Lily

This plant is indirectly light sensitive and moderately watered. Its dark green leaves are attractive even though it does not bloom as much during winter.

  • Rubber Plant

The leaves of rubber plants are wide and glossy, and they are green in winter. They are exposed to constant indoor temperature and diffused sun.

Simple winter care tips for indoor plants

Even healthy plants require basic care in winter. Nevertheless, care does not necessarily need to be complex.

  • Water when the soil is dry.
  • Store plants not near cold drafts and heaters.
  • Wipe leaves with a clean cloth to eliminate dust.
  • Plants should be positioned close to windows to get natural light.
  • Fertilizing in the middle of winter should be avoided.

However, rotating plants are also sometimes used to ensure that the light is distributed evenly across all sides. Even little actions such as these ensure the health of plants with minimum effort.

Frequently Asked Questions

  1. Which indoor plants survive best during winter?

Snake plant, pothos, ZZ plant, peace lily, and rubber plant remain green in the winter indoors.

  1. Do indoor plants need less water in winter?

Yes, plants do not grow as much in winter, meaning that watering less frequently will avoid root rot.

  1. Can indoor plants live with low sunlight in winter?

Several indoor plants can withstand low light, and they can still stay healthy even in winter.

  1. Are indoor plants good for the home environment in winter?

Yes, they make the air cleaner and less dry and make the inside environment more peaceful.
