How a Balcony Garden Helped Me Reconnect with Nature

2025-04-30

Table of Contents

  • Introduction
  • Why did I choose a balcony garden?
  • The benefits of a balcony garden
  • Choosing the right plants for my balcony
  • Tips for maintaining a balcony garden
  • Conclusion

Introduction

Gardening is always a quiet and fulfilling experience. However, because of the limited space in urban areas, acquiring space is a problem for a garden. Therefore, I concluded that I would begin a balcony garden. I did not realize that this small activity would help me rediscover the importance of living near nature.

Why did I choose a balcony garden?

As I move to a city like Indore, there are no more places where I can feel connected with nature. Additionally, due to my busy lifestyle, maintaining a garden is a little difficult, but I created a garden on my balcony, and this was the best solution for me. 

Having a garden on my balcony enabled me to have a view of the plants without going outside the house. It gave me a serene space where I could unwind and meditate. Besides, I was able to cultivate various types of plants, such as flowers, herbs, and vegetables.

The benefits of a balcony garden

Having a balcony garden brought a lot of good things to my life. These are some of the advantages:

  • Stress Relief: Taking time among the greenery soothes the mind and eases stress.
  • Improved Air Quality: The plants clean the air, providing a cleaner environment.
  • Connection with Nature: My balcony garden pushed nature to the forefront, making me feel more connected.
  • Home Aesthetic: Lovely plants added color to my home.

Choosing the right plants for my balcony

The most important thing about having a successful balcony garden is picking the perfect flowers. I studied and chose some flowers that will flourish during the summer in Indore.

  • Flowering Plants to Grow During the Indore Summer

Indore summers can get pretty hot, and therefore, having strong flowering plants is essential. Some of the best flowering plants for summer in Indore are given below:

  • Marigolds: Bright, yellow flowers that will make any area cheerful.
  • Zinnias: Tolerant flowers that open all summer long.
  • Petunias: Ideal for bright places, available in various colors. 

  • Best Summer Hanging Basket Plants in Indore

Indore hanging baskets are a great way to utilize space. Some of the best summer plants for hanging basket in Indore are.

  • Fuchsia: Known for their distinctive, bell-like flowers.
  • Lobelia: The flowering plant adds a bit of blue color to your balcony.
  • Impatiens: Vibrant flowers that tolerate indirect light.

  • Top Summer Plants for Home in Indore

While choosing plants for my balcony, I also thought about indoor plants for a refreshing atmosphere. The best summer plants for home in Indore are.

  • Aloe Vera: Low-maintenance and provides several health benefits.
  • Spider Plants: Can be used in both indoor and outdoor environments.
  • Succulents: Best for people who do not want to take care of plants frequently.

Tips for maintaining a balcony garden

Taking care of a balcony garden is not that difficult, but still worth the trouble. The following are a few things that I do:

  • Watering: During the summer season, plants require more water, so I always make sure my plant is well-watered. 
  • Pruning: For healthy growth, check out the plant; if required, then cut off the dead leaves or flowers.
  • Sunlight: Place your plants in a position where they would receive ample sunlight.
  • Soil Quality: Use quality soil so that your plants grow well.

I also ensured that I went to the best plant nursery in Indore so that I would get healthy plants. Additionally, I realized that a little care and attention are enough to keep my garden blooming all the time.

Conclusion

Having a balcony garden was one of my best choices. It connected me to interact with nature in a natural way, which also helped me to refresh. By making the right plants, such as the most beautiful flowering plants for Indore summer, I was able to enjoy a good and healthy garden. If you wish to have your balcony garden as well, then it would be more advisable to seek guidance from the best nursery in Indore.

So, ensure you plant what does well in your climatic conditions. If it is for decoration, then balcony gardening is a wonderful way of bringing nature into your world. Additionally, gardening has imparted satisfaction and patience to me. It is an easy-maintenance but fulfilling method of observing nature, even in the city.
